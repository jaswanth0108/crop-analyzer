More Than Just a Disease Detector

Most tools stop at identifying the disease. AgriShield goes further — it explains, quantifies, tracks, and guides.

Disease Classification

EfficientNet-B0 trained on Paddy Doctor & PlantVillage. Identifies 11 disease conditions across 3 crops with crop-type detection.

Grad-CAM Heatmap

Visual explanation of which leaf regions influenced the prediction. Builds farmer trust through transparent, explainable AI.

Severity Estimation

For rice diseases: U-Net segmentation estimates lesion coverage as % of leaf area. 6-tier scale from Mild to Critical.

Health Progression

Track crop health across multiple scans over time. Spot worsening conditions early with interactive Chart.js progression graphs.

Severity-Aware Guidance

Recommendations calibrated to severity level. No pesticide advice without expert sign-off. Safe, honest, and practical steps only.

Transparent & Honest

Low-confidence predictions flagged explicitly. Unsupported crops trigger "Unable to identify." No misleading claims. Ever.

⚠️ Known Limitations

•Severity estimation available only for rice diseases with lesion annotations. Tomato and potato display "Unavailable".
•PlantVillage images are largely studio images. Performance on real-field images with natural backgrounds may be lower.
•The model requires visible symptoms. Early-stage or microscopic disease detection is not supported.
•Near-duplicate images from training and test sets may affect accuracy metrics. Prevention steps are applied but not guaranteed.
•This demo uses a mock inference pipeline. All results are simulated. No real AI model is running.
